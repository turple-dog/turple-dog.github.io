# Privacy policy: Brick Breaker


**Effective date:** 25 November 2026  
**Who we are:** turple-dog, the independent developer of Brick Breaker ("we").  
**Contact for privacy requests:** games@emanthony.party

Brick Breaker ("the game") is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law says so), and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## What the game does with data

Most of the game runs on your phone. Your progress (the highest level and best score) is kept on the device in the app's own storage. We run **no server that receives your data**. The services below are provided by other companies and handle data under their own policies.

### Advertising: Google AdMob

The game shows interstitial and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect your **advertising ID**, **IP address** (from which it derives approximate location), device and OS information and ad interaction data. Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and some US states) the game asks for your consent first through Google's consent form; **without consent, only non-personalized ads can be shown, or none**. On iOS, personalized ads also need your permission in Apple's tracking prompt. You can change your choice any time in **Settings > Privacy options** in the game. Learn more: Google's privacy policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy, and "How Google uses data", https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. If you buy **Remove ads**, no ads are shown.

### Analytics: Firebase Analytics (Google)

If you agree (where consent is required), the game sends events such as the level you reached, your score, level duration, whether you used a continue, ad and purchase events (with price and currency) and notification responses, together with Firebase's app instance ID, device and OS information and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We don't set a user ID, and we configure Firebase with privacy-safe defaults (no advertising ID collection). Withdrawing consent in Privacy options stops it at once.

### Error reports: Sentry

If you agree, the game sends error reports (the error message, stack trace, device model, OS and app version) to Sentry, in its EU region. We remove anything that looks like personal data before sending.

### Purchases: Google Play and Apple App Store

Purchases (Remove ads) are processed by Google or Apple; we never see your payment details. The game checks your entitlement on your device and keeps a local record that you own it. Use **Restore purchases** in the settings on a new device.

### Sign-in, leaderboard and cloud save: Google Play Games / Apple Game Center and iCloud

If you sign in to Google Play Games or Game Center, the game submits your best score to the leaderboard (shown with your platform display name) and stores your progress in your Google or Apple cloud save. This is optional: the game works signed out. We use a hashed form of your platform's player ID inside the game. You manage and delete this data in your Google or Apple account.

### Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging

If you allow notifications, the game shows a local reminder and may receive messages through Firebase Cloud Messaging, which uses a device registration token. We address messages by topics built from the game, your language, platform and country, not by individual token. You can turn notifications off in the game (Settings) or in your phone's settings; turning them off in the game makes the push service forget the install.

### Remote settings

The game occasionally downloads a small signed settings file (for example ad limits) over HTTPS from a server we run. The request reveals your IP address to that host, like any web request. The random install number used for gradual rollouts is made and kept on your device and is never sent.

## Legal bases and your rights

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies: consent (advertising, analytics, crash reports and notifications), and performance of a contract (purchases and sign-in features). You may withdraw consent at any time in the game. You may ask to access, correct, delete or port your data, object to processing, or complain to your data-protection authority; for data held by Google, Apple or Sentry, use their tools too. California and other US state residents may have rights to know, delete and opt out of sale or sharing; you can opt out of personalized ads in Privacy options and in your phone's ad settings. We don't sell personal information for money.

## Retention, security, transfers

We hold no personal data ourselves. The vendors keep data under their own schedules (see their policies); Firebase Analytics keeps event data for 2 months, and Sentry keeps error reports for at most 90 days. Data travels over HTTPS. Vendors may process data outside your country, including in the US, under their own transfer safeguards.

## Children

The game is not directed at children. Where the consent flow identifies a player under the age of consent, ads are served in a child-directed way and we send no analytics. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us at games@emanthony.party.

## Changes

We'll update this page and its date when the data the game handles changes.
